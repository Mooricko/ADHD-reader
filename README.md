What Was Built:
Word-by-Word RSVP Center Stage:
Optical Center Lock (ORP): Mathematically anchors the highlighted middle letters of each word to the exact horizontal center line so the reader's eyes never make fatigue-inducing saccadic jumps.
Middle-Letter Highlighting: Highlights the exact two middle letters of each word in vivid crimson red (with optional customizable styles including single focal letter and bionic prefix, and color palettes like Amber, Emerald, Azure, Neon Violet, and Cyan).
Top & Bottom Focus Reticles: Subtle optical guide ticks directly pointing to the focal letters.
ADHD Rhythm & Pacing Engine:
Speed & WPM Control: Smooth speed slider and quick presets (200, 300, 450, 600 WPM) with dynamic +/-25 WPM shortcuts.
Smart Punctuation Pauses: Natural micro-pauses at periods, commas, semicolons, and paragraph breaks to prevent cognitive overload.
Rhythmic Audio Metronome: Optional Web Audio focus click/tap to anchor ADHD auditory rhythm.
Dual Reading Modes:
Word-by-Word (RSVP Flash Mode): Distraction-free single word stream with timeline scrubber, word counter, and time-remaining estimator.
Full Text Flow Mode: Full-article bionic view where every word in the document has its middle letters highlighted, with clickable jump-to-word and auto-scrolling reading tracker.
Text Management & Curated ADHD Library:
Direct paste from clipboard and .txt / .md file upload.
Built-in curated high-interest articles on ADHD neuroscience, hyperfocus, and deep work.
Local persistence for reading progress, saved clippings, and custom settings.
Accessibility & Focus Themes:
ADHD and dyslexia-friendly typography: Lexend, Atkinson Hyperlegible, JetBrains Mono, Newsreader Serif, and Plus Jakarta Sans.
High-contrast visual themes: Midnight Dark, OLED Pure Black, Warm Sepia, Nordic Slate, and Clean Light.
Full keyboard shortcuts support (Space to play/pause, Arrows for speed & jumping, R to restart, F for fullscreen, M to toggle modes)
